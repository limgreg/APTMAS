Fonts — replace the Noto Sans SC / JetBrains Mono @import at the top of
globals.css, or better, move to next/font in src/app/layout.tsx:

@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Archivo:wght@400;500;600;700;800&family=Barlow:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Barlow+Condensed:wght@500;600;700&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500&display=swap");

Note: the zh and ko locales still need a CJK face. Keep 'Noto Sans SC' in the
--font-sans stack after 'Barlow' so Chinese and Korean glyphs do not fall back to
a system serif:

  --font-sans: 'Barlow', 'Noto Sans SC', 'PingFang SC', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif;

Barlow has no CJK coverage, so CJK text will render in Noto Sans SC automatically.
Archivo and Barlow Condensed are Latin-only — for the big display numbers this is
fine (they are digits), but any CJK headline set in --font-display will fall back;
use --font-sans at a heavier weight for CJK headlines instead.
