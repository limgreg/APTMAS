# Scorecard verification

Generated by `python -m analysis.verify_scorecard`. Model ① against the panel's own
`total_score`. The scoring tables are extracted from the official PDF by
`analysis/extract_scoring_tables.py`; no threshold is entered by hand.

## Headline

- Rows scored: **144,236** (36,059 students × 4 years)
- Exact agreement, all rows: **83.8%**
- Exact agreement, clean rows: **88.4%** (n=116,456)
- Within ±1 point, clean rows: **93.2%**

## What is structurally confirmed

These were identified *from the data*, not assumed:

- **Item weights.** Regressing the recorded total on our per-item scores (OLS, no
  intercept, 20k rows) recovers 15/15/20/10/10/20/10 — the PDF's weights — with
  R² ≈ 0.99 in every measurement year.
- **Grade-group mapping.** Scoring g1/g2 on the 大一大二 table and g3/g4 on 大三大四
  matches at 0.88/0.70/0.85/0.89; forcing the wrong group collapses agreement to
  0.03–0.07. Decisive.
- **Endurance thresholds.** Reconstructing the implied threshold for each score level
  from the data reproduces the PDF's printed values exactly at every level with solid
  support (males 大一大二; the same holds for the other three sex × grade-group
  tables). Literal thresholds are deliberately not reproduced here — they live only in
  the git-ignored `data/scoring_tables/` (see that directory's README).
- **Total = Σ(score × weight) + 附加分, rounded to 1dp, not capped at 100.** Recorded
  totals reach 104.4, and 1dp rounding raises agreement from 78.3% to 83.8%.

## Where it diverges, and why

| Subset | Rows | Exact |
|---|---:|---:|
| All | 144,236 | 83.8% |
| Winsorised values (excluded) | 23,464 | 76.0% |
| 2018 g2 cell (excluded) | 4,444 | 1.3% |
| Clean | 116,456 | 88.4% |

**Winsorisation.** The panel was pre-cleaned before it reached us. Every item column
has a shared extreme with an implausible pile-up (1,821 rows at exactly 185.0 s
endurance; 1,573 at 1238 ml vital capacity; 1,874 at 6.5 s sprint). The recorded total
was computed from the original values, so clipped rows cannot reproduce it. A sibling
source file is even named 「初步删掉极值」.

**The 2018-g2 cell.** The 2017 intake's second sitting
matches at ~1% while every other (year, grade) cell matches at 79–94%, and its totals
carry 0.05 granularity where the rest are rounded to 0.1. This looks like a different
scoring policy for that sitting — consistent with the team's own `policy_compare_g1.csv`
and `covid_compare_g3.csv`. **Mentor question:** which standard applied in 2018?

**Residual.** BMI in the panel disagrees with weight/height² by >0.5 for ~2% of rows,
so the BMI column is itself derived and has drifted. This bounds how exactly any
re-derivation can match.

## Reading this honestly

The Scorecard is **exact with respect to the published standard** — that is what makes
it usable as ground truth for attribution and counterfactuals. Disagreement with the
recorded totals is attributable to upstream data handling (winsorisation, a derived BMI
column, one anomalous sitting), not to an approximated threshold. No value in
`data/scoring_tables/` was entered by hand.
