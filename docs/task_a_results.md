## 2. The headline results

### The scoring tables are parsed, not transcribed

`analysis/extract_scoring_tables.py` reads the official PDF and asserts structural invariants —
20 score levels in order, weights summing to exactly 100, strictly monotonic thresholds,
gap-free BMI categories. It **aborts rather than emitting a table it cannot verify**. The
single auditable origin of every scored number is the PDF plus that script; nothing is typed
in by hand, which is what `AGENTS.md` §The core premise requires.

Two readings the PDF's text layer left ambiguous were resolved by evidence, not assumption:

- **BMI 超重 = 80.** The score cell is vertically merged across 低体重 and 超重. Two independent
  extractions confirm it: `pdfplumber` attaches the `80` to the first spanned row, layout-mode
  extraction centres it on the second.
- **BMI categories are anchored at printed lower bounds.** The standard prints bounds to one
  decimal (低体重 ≤17.8, 正常 17.9–23.9), but measured BMI is continuous — a real student at
  17.8439 falls in none of the printed intervals. Anchoring at lower bounds tiles the line with
  no gap and preserves every printed boundary.

### The Scorecard reproduces the cohort's own totals

**88.4% exact on clean rows** (n=116,456), 93.2% within one point; 83.8% exact across all
144,236 student-years. Four facts about the scoring rule were **identified from the data** and
each matched the PDF:

| Claim | How it was established |
|---|---|
| Weights 15/15/20/10/10/20/10 | OLS of recorded total on our item scores, no intercept, recovers them with R² ≈ 0.99 in every measurement year |
| g1/g2 → 大一大二, g3/g4 → 大三大四 | Natural mapping matches at 0.88/0.70/0.85/0.89; forcing the wrong group collapses to 0.03–0.07 |
| Endurance thresholds | Reconstructing the implied threshold per score level from the data reproduces the printed values exactly |
| Total = Σ(score × weight) + 附加分, rounded to 1dp, **not capped at 100** | Recorded totals reach 104.4; 1dp rounding lifts agreement from 78.3% to 83.8% |

Divergence is attributed, not hidden — see §3.

### The scholarship pass rule, resolved from data

`pass_scholarship` is exactly **evaluation-year total ≥ 60.0**. Lowest passing score 60.0,
highest failing score 59.9, **100% agreement across all 20,140 students**. This closes an open
question the plan listed as blocking, and it was derived rather than assumed
(`analysis/route_to_pass.verify_pass_threshold`).

### Route-to-Pass, and who it refuses to advise

Exact search over the scoring function, ranked by required improvement in cohort standard
deviations. Endurance dominates the cheapest routes, matching the known g1→g4 decline.

**16.8% of failing students have no route to a pass within the safety cap** and are escalated
to a PE teacher instead of being shown an unsafe target. BMI is excluded from routes entirely —
a "BMI route" is weight-loss advice under another name.

### Progress Check

AUC **0.866** from first-year features alone, **0.872** from the first two years, against a
0.737 majority baseline. Train and test AUC are within 0.004, so it is not overfitting.
Predicting a *later* outcome from *earlier* features is enforced in `build_dataset()`, which
raises if the feature years are not strictly earlier than the evaluation year — predicting
same-year `total_score` from same-year items would be arithmetic, not modelling.

### Student Types

Four interpretable segments, each with a distinct headroom item. The one the system exists for —
**broad headroom**, n=21,990, mean total 51.7, 77.5% under the gate — is identified and
characterised. Profiling uses **item scores rather than raw values**, because the standard has
already normalised for sex and grade group: 30 sit-ups and 10 pull-ups are not comparable, but
the points each earns are.

### Trust Check — the number that belongs next to every attribution claim

Because the total is a *known* linear function of the item scores, explanation fidelity is
measurable rather than assumed. SHAP recovers the published weights to **0.006 mean absolute
error** (sit_and_reach 0.0996 vs 0.10; strength 0.0999 vs 0.10) with **0.998 per-row
attribution correlation**, on a model that fits the known function at R² 0.997.

That earns the attributions in A2 a measured amount of trust — and it is the concrete form of
the project's differentiator.

## 3. Things we found that the team should know

These are findings about the **data and method**, not the students. Each one changed how
something was built.

1. **The panel is winsorised.** Every item column has a shared extreme with an implausible
   pile-up: 1,821 rows at exactly 185.0 s endurance, 1,573 at 1238 ml vital capacity, 1,874 at
   6.5 s sprint, 1,182 at −7.3 cm sit-and-reach. Those are not the values the recorded totals
   were computed from, which accounts for much of the Scorecard divergence. A sibling source
   file is named 「初步删掉极值」.
2. **The 2018 g2 sitting follows a different rule.** The 2017 intake's second year matches at
   ~1% while every other (year, grade) cell matches at 79–94%, and its totals carry 0.05
   granularity where the rest are rounded to 0.1. **Mentor question.**
3. **The SZU export's 腰臀比 column is corrupt** — a verbatim duplicate of 基础代谢率 in all 297
   rows, with BMR-scale values (962–2267) rather than a ratio. The waist-hip ratio is declared
   in the catalogue as unavailable rather than silently dropped, and the loader refuses to emit
   it. **Re-request from mentors.**
4. **A fixed SHAP importance threshold is below the noise floor.** A *pure random* feature
   earns 1.3–2.2% of total SHAP importance — gradient-boosted trees give continuous noise
   plenty of usable split points. The A3 mechanism's original 1% cutoff would have kept noise
   while pruning two genuine features, so the bar is now measured each refit with injected
   random probes.
5. **`enrollment_year` is the largest single Progress Check driver.** Part of the model's skill
   is cohort effects, not fitness. It is kept as a control (removing it would push its signal
   into the fitness features) but marked `actionable: false` so no guidance can ever be built
   on it. Same for `is_male`.
6. **`is_male` sits near the noise floor, and that is the *expected* result** — the standard
   already scores each sex on its own table, so sex is largely encoded in the item scores.
7. **The SZU pilot is far below WHO guidance**: 84.5% of the 297 respondents fall under
   150 min/week of moderate activity; only 12.8% are inside the 150–300 min band.

## 5. How to run it

Real data is git-ignored. With the files in `data/`:

```bash
pip install -e ".[analysis,dev]"

python -m analysis.extract_scoring_tables   # PDF -> data/scoring_tables/ (do this first)
python -m analysis.verify_scorecard         # ① verification report
python -m analysis.indicators               # A1 catalogue
python -m analysis.student_types            # ④ segments + weakness profiles
python -m analysis.progress_check           # ③ risk model
python -m analysis.rule_base                # A2 SHAP + LIME + expert
python -m analysis.dynamic_update           # A3 replay + self-test
python -m analysis.trust_check              # ⑧ explanation fidelity
python -m analysis.handoff                  # synthetic fixtures for Task B

pytest                                      # 46 tests
```

Reports land in `analysis/reports/` (markdown + JSON + figures). Synthetic Task B fixtures land
in `data/synthetic/handoff_fixtures.json` and are safe to commit — they are generated students
scored through the real engine, containing no real record.

Teammates who only have the CSVs and not the PDF will get a clear
`ScoringTablesUnavailable` telling them what to run; tests skip rather than fail when data is
absent, so a fresh clone is still green.

## 6. What Task B can rely on today

- The **schema in `task_b_handoff.md` §2 is implemented** and validated by
  `tests/test_handoff_contract.py`.
- **Synthetic fixtures exist now** — no waiting on real data or on consent.
- **Provenance ids are enforced**, not merely promised: `validate_handoff()` rejects an object
  with missing or duplicate ids, and the contract test drives the real
  `aptams.agent.provenance.validate_grounding` to show that a sentence citing an id the object
  does not carry is refused.
- **The privacy boundary is enforced server-side** in `handoff.for_role()`: a teacher view drops
  every `teacher_visible: false` indicator and returns only a non-specific count of what was
  withheld, never the values.
- **Certainty is typed** so the agent's wording can match it: `score` exact, `route` exact but
  `causal: false` with `effort_is_placeholder`, `progress` fuzzy with an explicit `uncertainty`
  string.
